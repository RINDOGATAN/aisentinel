AI governance frameworks wheel

Twelve widely used AI governance frameworks compared on fourteen dimensions, with a picker that suggests which ones to start from for a given situation.

As of 2026-09-16A learning aid, not legal advice.

12 frameworks, 14 dimensions, 168 cells, 23 marked "to verify". Each cell was checked against the official text where the official site could be read on the as-of date; cells that could not be checked say "to verify" and are drawn hatched. Depth is an editorial reading of how far the framework goes on that dimension, not a score of quality.

      Answer six questions. The page lists the frameworks that apply or fit, ranked, each with one line of reasoning, and suggests a stack: the binding laws, one management system and one risk method.

      Why this result: the rules

      Each rule adds its score to a framework when every listed condition matches (any of the listed values). Binding laws rank first, then by score. The stack takes the first matching rule in each list.
